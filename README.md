# SuperNova 🌌

**A fast, private browser with a futuristic, gaming-inspired look — free to use.**

SuperNova is built on Chromium, so websites work just like they do in Chrome or Edge.
Everything around them is SuperNova's own: built-in ad blocking, workspaces, a sidebar
for your apps, deep customization and a lot more.

## ✨ Highlights

- 🛡️ **Ad & tracker blocking built in** — including YouTube video ads. No extension needed.
- 🚨 **Scam & pop-up protection** — warns you before known scam, malware and phishing sites.
- 🗂️ **Workspaces & Tab Islands** — separate sets of tabs (work, gaming, school…) and colored, collapsible tab groups.
- 📐 **Split view** — two tabs side by side; drag a tab onto the page and hold it.
- 📌 **Sidebar** — Discord, Spotify, WhatsApp and your own favorites always one click away.
- 🎨 **Make it yours** — 6 themes or your own, 14 animated New Tab wallpapers, and look-alike designs of Opera GX, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari.
- 🔐 **Vault** — encrypted passwords, cards and addresses with autofill, protected by your PIN.
- 🔒 **Owner lock** — a PIN or password before anything opens (can be turned off).
- 🎮 **Game Mode & Performance monitor** — see what uses your CPU and memory, put tabs to sleep, set limits.
- 📝 **Notes, Tools & Nova Lens** — notes with Markdown, a calculator, QR scanner, color picker, and copying text out of images.
- 🌍 **Translate pages** — in English, Norwegian, German, Spanish and French.
- 🕹️ **No internet? Play a game** — Nova Runner, Snake and 2048 instead of an error page.

## 📥 Install

1. Download **`SuperNova-Setup-1.x.x.exe`** below.
2. Run it. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** — this appears because SuperNova
   is new and not signed with a paid certificate. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Choose where to install it, and you're done.

**Coming from another browser?** Import your bookmarks from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Opera GX
or Vivaldi (Bookmarks → Import), and your passwords from a CSV export (Vault → Import).

## 🔄 Updates

SuperNova updates itself. When a new version is out, a banner appears at the top —
click **Install and restart** and you're on the new version. Every update is digitally
signed and checked before it is installed.

## 🔏 Privacy

Your bookmarks, history, passwords and settings stay **on your computer**. SuperNova has no
account and doesn't collect or send your data. Uninstalling keeps your data, so a reinstall
picks up where you left off.

## 💻 Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
